Company Handbook

Work Arrangement
Employees follow a hybrid work schedule. Office attendance is required on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Monday and Friday may be worked remotely with manager approval.

Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 30 days of annual paid leave. Leave accrues monthly and must be approved before use.

Technology Standards
Backend services are developed using Python.
Frontend applications use React.
Mobile applications use React Native.

Security
Employees must use multi-factor authentication for company accounts and must not share passwords.

Support
Technical issues should be reported through the internal support portal.
